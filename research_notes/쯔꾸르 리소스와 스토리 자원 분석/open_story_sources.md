# Open Story Resources & Narrative Design Toolkit for a 2D Horror/Mystery Platformer (ACTION GAME MAKER / Godot, GPL-3.0 repo)

Research date: 2026-11-16. Scope: legally reusable story source material (Korean / Japanese / Western), licence interaction with a GPL-3.0 repo and a commercial release, narrative-design frameworks, narrative tools, and concrete "story seeds".
Legend used below: **PD** = public domain (no copyright); **free-to-read** = online access only, reuse NOT granted; **KOGL-N** = 공공누리 type N; **CC BY-SA** = Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike. Nothing here is legal advice.

## Q1. Korean public-domain / open-licence sources (folklore, encyclopedias, archives, modern literature)

### Takeaway
Korean folk motifs themselves (plots, characters such as 장화홍련, 바리공주, 구미호, 도깨비, 저승사자) are safe to adapt, but the *specific texts* that record them are not automatically free: the 한국민족문화대백과사전 is usable "under 공공누리" without a stated type on the article page, the licence of 한국구비문학대계 transcripts could not be confirmed, and KOGL type 3/4 forbid adaptation. Korean modern-literature authors who died **in or before 1962** are PD; those who died **1963+** are protected for life+70 (e.g., 염상섭 until 2033), so no new author-death-based PD enters Korea until 2034.

### Cited Findings

**Korean copyright term rules**
- Current Korean law: economic rights last for the author's life + 70 years (저작권법 제39조) — [korea.legal 저작권법 제39조](https://korea.legal/wiki/%ec%a0%80%ec%9e%91%ea%b6%8c%eb%b2%95-%ec%a0%9c39%ec%a1%b0/)
- MCST explains the term was life+50 and became life+70 from 2013-07-01 — [문화체육관광부 저작권 상식](https://www.mcst.go.kr/kor/s_policy/copyright/knowledge/know01.jsp)
- Authors who died in or before 1962 had their 50-year term expire by end-2012 (before the 2013-07-01 extension), so their works are free; e.g., Hermann Hesse (d. 1962) expired end-2012, while 염상섭 (d. 1963) is protected until 2033 — [blog summary of the transitional rule (secondary source)](https://taezapresents.blogspot.com/2023/12/blog-post.html). Note: this is a secondary source; the supplementary provision (부칙) text itself was not retrieved.
- MCST/KCC say expired works can be checked on 공유마당 — same blog citing MCST; and 공유마당 "만료저작물" are described as usable by anyone without permission — [한국저작권위원회 공유마당 안내](https://www.copyright.or.kr/business/gongu/index.do)

**공유마당 (gongu.copyright.or.kr) categories**
- 만료저작물 (expired works): usable freely without separate permission; 기증저작물 "자유이용" type requires indicating work/author/source/conditions; "조건부이용" donations need staff approval; CCL works follow BY/NC/ND/SA conditions; KOGL works follow their 공공누리 type — [한국저작권위원회 공유마당](https://www.copyright.or.kr/business/gongu/index.do)

**공공누리 (KOGL) types — exact conditions (MCST)**
- Type 1: attribution; commercial and non-commercial use; modification / derivative works allowed — [MCST 공공누리 유형](https://www.mcst.go.kr/kor/s_open/kogl/koglType.jsp?pTab=02)
- Type 2: attribution + no commercial use ("비영리 목적으로만 이용 가능"); derivatives allowed — [MCST](https://www.mcst.go.kr/kor/s_open/kogl/koglType.jsp?pTab=02)
- Type 3: attribution + no modification; commercial OK; the ban includes "번역·편곡·각색·영상제작 등을 위해 2차적 저작물을 작성하는 것" — [MCST](https://www.mcst.go.kr/kor/s_open/kogl/koglType.jsp?pTab=02)
- Type 4: attribution + non-commercial + no modification — [MCST](https://www.mcst.go.kr/kor/s_open/kogl/koglType.jsp?pTab=02)
- Expired public works: "이용조건(출처표시 포함) 없음" — [MCST](https://www.mcst.go.kr/kor/s_open/kogl/koglType.jsp?pTab=02)
- Newer types exist: 제0유형 (no attribution condition, commercial + derivatives allowed) and an "AI유형" for AI training — [한국저작권위원회 공공누리 안내](https://copyright.or.kr/gov/nuri/rule_info/index.do) (via search summary; page not fetched in full)
- Under type 2, commercial use "영리행위와 직접 또는 간접으로 관련된 행위" is barred unless separate permission is obtained — [MCST via search summary](https://www.mcst.go.kr/kor/s_open/kogl/koglType.jsp?pTab=03)
- Individual agencies add attribution requirements (e.g., 하남시 requires year, agency name, URL and author name for every type) — [하남시 저작권보호 정책](https://www.hanam.go.kr/www/contents.do?key=2259)

**한국민족문화대백과사전 (EncyKorea, AKS)**
- Article footer: "한국민족문화대백과사전은 공공저작물로서 공공누리 제도에 따라 이용 가능합니다"; media items carry individual 공공누리 marks; required citation format "[출처 : 항목명 - 한국민족문화대백과사전]". The article page did NOT state a type number for the text — [EncyKorea 「한국구비문학대계」 항목](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0061076)

**한국구비문학대계 (Academy of Korean Studies)**
- Original series 1980–1992 (85 vols per infobox; body text says 82 books + supplements), fieldwork 1979–1985 in 78 of 182 target districts; contains 설화 14,941편, 민요 5,922편, 무가 375편; digitized holdings incl. new surveys: 설화 56,386건, 민요 23,454건, 무가 472건; 증편 56 vols (2013–2018); web: gubi.aks.ac.kr — [EncyKorea](https://encykorea.aks.ac.kr/Article/E0061076)
- A 2025 blog points to a newer address kdp.aks.ac.kr/inde/gubi (unofficial, unverified) — [blog](https://seoulzian.tistory.com/23)
- Reuse terms for the transcripts themselves: **not found** (see Gaps).

**국립민속박물관 한국민속대백과사전**
- Content is available on the web dictionary (folkency.nfm.go.kr) and, under the open-data policy, free via 공공데이터포털 (data.go.kr); 한국민속문학사전 is one of its 8 topics — [스포츠경향 2021](https://sports.khan.co.kr/article/202101141532003). KOGL type for the text: not confirmed (site returned 503 when fetched).

**Primary texts of folk/classical narratives**
- 장화홍련전: ko.wikisource hosts an index of the 1915 동명서관 edition (author 미상, editor listed as 고영수) but the text layer still needs OCR — [위키문헌 색인: 장화홍련전 (동명서관, 1915)](https://ko.wikisource.org/wiki/%EC%83%89%EC%9D%B8:%EC%9E%A5%ED%99%94%ED%99%8D%EB%A0%A8%EC%A0%84_(%EB%8F%99%EB%AA%85%EC%84%9C%EA%B4%80,_1915).djvu)
- Plot (for adaptation planning): stepmother frames Janghwa with a skinned rat as a fake miscarriage; Janghwa drowned in a pond; Hongryeon found in the same pond; each new village magistrate is found dead the day after arriving; a brave magistrate stays, his candle goes out, the ghosts tell him to cut open the "fetus" — it is a rat; the father later has twin girls named Janghwa and Hongryeon — [Wikipedia: Janghwa Hongryeon jeon](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janghwa_Hongryeon_jeon) (Wikipedia text itself is CC BY-SA — use for research, do not copy prose)
- 바리공주: an orally transmitted shamanic epic (서사무가) about the heroine becoming a shaman — [Koreascience paper](https://koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO201415640234763.do); she is the abandoned 7th daughter who goes alone to the underworld (저승) to obtain medicine for her sick father, revives her parents and becomes a deity — [Koreascience 2021 study](https://koreascience.or.kr/article/JAKO202108257607015.pdf); her underworld journey evokes anxiety and fear and the text deals with emotions provoked by death (compared with 차사본풀이) — [여성문학연구 2021 (accesson)](https://www.accesson.kr/femcritic/v.0/35/69/41783)

### Inferences
- **Ideas vs. expression:** a folk motif (ghost sisters, abandoned 7th daughter, 저승사자, 구미호) is not owned by anyone; a specific modern retelling, transcription, annotation or translation can be. Write your own retellings from multiple summaries rather than copying any single modern text (including EncyKorea prose and Wikipedia prose).
- **Safe Korean modern-literature list (died ≤1962 → PD):** 나도향 (d. 1926), 김소월 (d. 1934), 이상 (d. 1937), 김유정 (d. 1937), 이효석 (d. 1942), 현진건 (d. 1943), 윤동주 (d. 1945), 채만식 (d. 1950). Death years are from general knowledge — confirm each on 공유마당 before use. 염상섭 (d. 1963) is NOT PD until 2034 (cited above).
- Because only authors who died ≤1962 lapsed before the 2013 extension, nothing new enters Korean PD on author-death grounds until 2034-01-01 (authors d. 1963 → protected through 2033). So "2026 new PD" in Korea = none (inference from the cited rule).
- Practical rule for KOGL: only **type 0 / type 1** (and expired works) are usable for a commercial, adapted game; type 2 blocks sale; types 3/4 block adaptation entirely. Treat "공공누리에 따라 이용 가능" without a type number as **unconfirmed** until the type mark is seen on the specific page.
- Encyclopedias (EncyKorea, 한국민속대백과) are best used as **fact research** (motif lists, regional variants, ritual details) rather than as text to paste into the game.

## Q2. Japanese public-domain sources (Aozora Bunko, Hearn, Yanagita, Ranpo, Yumeno)

### Takeaway
Aozora Bunko files of PD works may be copied, redistributed (even for a fee) and adapted ("翻案など自由に活用"); it only *requests* that 底本 info be kept. Japan moved to life+70 on 2018-12-30 without reviving expired works, so authors who died ≤1967 are PD in Japan (e.g., 江戸川乱歩, d. 1965, PD since 2016) and nothing new by author death enters Japanese PD until 2039. Translations (e.g., Japanese translations of Hearn) carry their own translator copyright.

### Cited Findings
- Aozora rules for PD works: "ファイルは、有償・無償であるかを問わず、自由に複製・再配布・共有することができます" and "ファイルを元に、実演・口述・翻案など自由に活用することもできます"; Aozora *hopes* file info incl. 底本 is not deleted and that changes are recorded; "青空文庫" may be named as source — [青空文庫 収録ファイルの取り扱い規準](https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html)
- Works still in copyright on Aozora: copying only within private use (Art. 30); otherwise permission or the stated CC licence is needed; a translation stays protected while either author's or translator's rights last — [青空文庫 規準](https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html)
- Licence update for bibliographic data effective 2022-01-01 (CC BY 2.1 JP → CC BY 4.0) — [青空文庫 規準](https://www.aozora.gr.jp/guide/kijyunn.html); [ITmedia report](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/articles/1901/12/news020.html)
- Japan extended to life+70 from 2018-12-30 (TPP11); the extension covers people who died in 1968 or later (a 1968 death is protected to 2038-12-31); expired works are not revived — [ITmedia 2019](https://www.itmedia.co.jp/news/spv/1901/17/news105.html); [Impress Watch](https://www.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/news/1152314.html)
- 江戸川乱歩 entered PD in Japan in 2016 (50 years after death) and Aozora began publishing e.g. 怪人二十面相 from April 2016 — [INTERNET Watch](https://internet.watch.impress.co.jp/docs/yajiuma/731865.html); [J-CAST 2015](https://www.j-cast.com/2015/10/06247169.html?p=all)
- 遠野物語 is a collection of folk tales and strange (ghost) stories of the Tōno region (NDL record of a Shinchosha edition) — [NDL Search](https://ndlsearch.ndl.go.jp/en/books/R100000002-I027299197)
- Hearn's *Kwaidan: Stories and Studies of Strange Things* is on Project Gutenberg (#1210) — [Project Gutenberg #1210](https://www.gutenberg.org/etext/1210)
- Modern Japanese translations of Kwaidan exist (e.g., 南條竹則 trans., 光文社古典新訳文庫) — these are copyrighted translations — [紀伊國屋](https://www.kinokuniya.co.jp/f/dsg-01-9784334753801)

### Inferences
- **Status table (verify each):** 小泉八雲/Lafcadio Hearn (d. 1904) – English originals PD everywhere; 柳田國男 (d. 1962) – PD in Japan (50-year term expired end-2012) and also PD under Korea's "died ≤1962" rule; 夢野久作 (d. 1936) – PD; 江戸川乱歩 (d. 1965) – PD in Japan (cited) but under a straight Korean life+70 reading would run to 2035 unless Korea applies a rule of the shorter term to Japanese works → **treat Ranpo as "PD in Japan, Korean status to confirm"** before commercial adaptation sold in Korea. (Death years: general knowledge.)
- For Hearn, adapt from the **English 1904 original** (PD) rather than a Japanese translation whose translator may still be protected.
- Yokai lore in general (kappa, rokurokubi, yuki-onna) is folklore = free motifs; specific modern encyclopedias/illustrations (e.g., post-war yokai artists) are not.
- "2026 new PD" in Japan by author death: none (inference from 1968+ deaths being protected to 2038+).

### Gaps
- Aozora card pages for 遠野物語 / Hearn translations (translator death years such as 田部隆次) were not retrieved.
- Whether Korean law applies the rule of the shorter term to Japanese works (affects Ranpo) — unconfirmed.

## Q3. Western / global public-domain & open sources (Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Wikisource, Lovecraft, Poe, M.R. James, Kafka, SCP, Wikipedia)

### Takeaway
Project Gutenberg texts are PD in the US, but the "Project Gutenberg" *trademark* licence (royalties for commercial use) attaches unless you strip all PG references; Standard Ebooks dedicates its own production work to CC0, making it the cleanest English source. US PD advanced to **1930 works on 2026-01-01** (e.g., *The Maltese Falcon*, Kafka's *The Castle* in English, Christie's *The Murder at the Vicarage*) — but US PD ≠ Korean PD for authors who died after 1962. Lovecraft's status is disputed in the US but PD under life+70 regimes since 2008. SCP Foundation is CC BY-SA 3.0 with image exceptions (SCP-173's former photo is not CC).

### Cited Findings
**Project Gutenberg**
- If you strip the licence and all references to Project Gutenberg, "you are left with a text unrestricted by U.S. intellectual property law" — [Project Gutenberg License](https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license) (via search snippet; site returned 503 during fetch)
- Using the "Project Gutenberg" name (registered trademark) for commercial use requires royalties; the mark may not be used if you charge for an eBook except under the trademark licence — [PG Permissions](https://gutenberg.org/policy/permission.html); [PG licence text in an eBook](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/8292.kindle.noimages)
- Debian discussion: the PG licence is not DFSG-free because of commercial-use restrictions (on the trademark-bearing files) — [debian-devel 2025-03](https://lists.debian.org/debian-devel/2025/03/msg00229.html)
- PG texts are labelled PD in the USA (e.g., M.R. James *Ghost Stories of an Antiquary*, #8486) — [Gutenberg #8486](https://www.gutenberg.org/etext/8486)

**Standard Ebooks**
- "Content produced by or for Standard Ebooks L3C is dedicated to the public domain" (CC0 1.0) — [Standard Ebooks PD Day 2026 post](https://standardebooks.org/blog/public-domain-day-2026)
- 2026 releases (1930 works) include Kafka *The Castle*, Hammett *The Maltese Falcon*, Christie *The Murder at the Vicarage*, Sayers *Strong Poison*, du Maurier *Short Fiction* ("dark and sinister themes, often tinged with a paranormal flavor"), Carolyn Keene *The Hidden Staircase* (hauntings) etc. — [Standard Ebooks](https://standardebooks.org/blog/public-domain-day-2026)
- Standard Ebooks also has M.R. James *Short Fiction* compiling four collections incl. *Ghost Stories of an Antiquary* — [Standard Ebooks: M. R. James](https://standardebooks.org/ebooks/m-r-james/short-fiction)

**US Public Domain Day 2026**
- On 2026-01-01, works published in 1930 and sound recordings from 1925 entered the US public domain (US only) — [Duke CSPD Public Domain Day 2026](https://web.law.duke.edu/cspd/publicdomainday/2026/)
- Films like *All Quiet on the Western Front* (1930), Hitchcock's *Murder!*, and Betty Boop (Fleischer) also entered — [Texas Standard](https://texasstandard.org/stories/public-domain-day-2026-betty-boop-pluto-maltese-movies-books-art-music/); [Duke](https://web.law.duke.edu/cspd/publicdomainday/2026/)

**Lovecraft**
- Pre-1923 works PD in the US; many later works may have lapsed for non-renewal, but Lovecraft Properties LLC still asserts claims; non-renewal records are not conclusive proof — [Techdirt 2011](https://www.techdirt.com/articles/20110531/02515814477/confusing-case-lovecrafts-copyrights.shtml); [lovecraftr copyright note](https://jrdnbradford.github.io/lovecraftr/articles/copyright-status.html)
- All Lovecraft works entered PD in the EU in 2008 (life+70) — [Techdirt](https://www.techdirt.com/articles/20110531/02515814477/confusing-case-lovecrafts-copyrights.shtml)
- Wikisource hosts Lovecraft texts with per-work PD templates (e.g., PD-US-no-renewal category) — [Wikisource: H. P. Lovecraft](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Author:H._P._Lovecraft); [Category:PD-US-no-renewal](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Category:PD-US-no-renewal)

**SCP Foundation**
- Content is CC BY-SA 3.0; derived works "MUST" be released under CC BY-SA 3.0; attribution must include work name, author/alias, and a link to source (credit article + wiki) — [SCP Wiki Licensing Guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide)
- On games: "we cannot give specific legal advice regarding how to comply with the license while making a game"; you can "license your game under GPLv3, which is compatible with our license" — [SCP Licensing Guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide)
- Selling remixes is allowed ("you probably will not get rich off of them") — [SCP Licensing Guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide)
- Exception: "Do not sell anything related to SCP-173's former image, Izumi Kato's Untitled 2004 … not released under Creative Commons"; SCP-173's text is CC BY-SA 3.0; images governed by a separate Image Use Policy — [SCP Licensing Guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide)

### Inferences
- **Always strip PG headers/footers** (or start from Standard Ebooks / Wikisource) before putting any PD text into a commercial build.
- **US-PD vs Korea-PD check (by author death year — general knowledge, verify):** Poe (d. 1849), Grimm brothers (d. 1859/1863), M.R. James (d. 1936), Lovecraft (d. 1937), Kafka (d. 1924; German original), Hammett (d. 1961), Faulkner (d. 1962), Sayers (d. 1957) → PD in Korea under the "died ≤1962" rule. **Christie (d. 1976), du Maurier (d. 1989), Mildred Wirt Benson/"Carolyn Keene" (d. 2002)** → US-PD for 1930 titles but still protected in Korea/UK/Japan → avoid for a globally sold game. **Borges (d. 1986)** → NOT PD (life+70 → 2056).
- Translations matter: Kafka's German original is PD; the 1930 English translation (Willa & Edwin Muir) is US-PD per Standard Ebooks but likely still protected under life+70 elsewhere (Willa Muir d. 1970 — general knowledge) → translate from German yourself or write an original retelling.
- Lovecraft: for safety use only works first published before 1931 (US PD by publication date as of 2026, regardless of renewal disputes), e.g., *The Call of Cthulhu* (1928), *The Colour Out of Space* (1927) — publication years from general knowledge; avoid "Cthulhu Mythos" trademarks/branding of third-party RPG products.
- SCP: if you want a proprietary or GPL-only narrative, avoid SCP text; the "SCP" brand and Foundation logo usage policies were not verified here.

- SCP-096 image history / other image exceptions — not covered by the Licensing Guide page; check the SCP Image Use Policy.

## Q4. How CC BY-SA story material interacts with a GPL-3.0 code repo and a commercial game

### Takeaway
ShareAlike applies to **adaptations** of the BY-SA material, not to a mere **collection** of separate works; a game that contains BY-SA-derived story text plus independently made art/music/code is arguably a collection, but whether a narrative game as a whole is an "adaptation" is legally untested and SCP explicitly declines to advise. CC BY-SA 4.0 is officially one-way compatible with GPLv3 (BY-SA adaptations may be released under GPLv3, not vice-versa), which fits a GPL-3.0 repo; commercial sale is allowed under both, but you cannot stop others from redistributing the BY-SA/GPL parts.

### Cited Findings
- SA "only applies if a work is modified and if the work is shared publicly"; CC licences "do not require the collection or the compilation itself to be made available under an SA license"; choosing a collection licence "does not change the license applicable to the original material" — [Creative Commons FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/)
- A modification becomes an adaptation when "based on the prior work" with new creativity; synching music to moving image is always an adaptation — [CC FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/)
- "We recommend against using Creative Commons licenses for software"; CC licences "may be used for … separate artistic elements such as game art or music" — [CC FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/)
- A CC licence "only speaks to copyright" (other rights such as personality/privacy may still apply) — [CC FAQ](https://creativecommons.org/faq/)
- CC declared GPLv3 a BY-SA 4.0-compatible licence in 2015 (one-way); framed as serving games and hardware; reusers of the combined work "need only look to the conditions of GPLv3" — [CC blog 2015-10-08](https://creativecommons.org/2015/10/08/cc-by-sa-4-0-now-one-way-compatible-with-gplv3/); [CC wiki: ShareAlike compatibility: GPLv3](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/ShareAlike_compatibility:_GPLv3)
- Compatibility is only with GPLv3 (not GPLv2) — [CC wiki](https://wiki.creativecommons.org/wiki/ShareAlike_compatibility:_GPLv3)
- SCP's guide simultaneously says derivatives must stay CC BY-SA 3.0 ("You simply can't change the license") and that a game can be GPLv3 "compatible with our license" — [SCP Licensing Guide](https://scp-wiki.wikidot.com/licensing-guide) (internal tension; CC's official compatibility is defined for BY-SA **4.0**, while SCP is **3.0**)

### Inferences
- **Recommended layering for this repo:** (1) code = GPL-3.0 (already chosen); (2) your *original* story text/art = any licence you choose (can keep proprietary rights in separate asset packs if you are the sole author, or CC BY-SA 4.0 / GPL-3.0 if you want openness); (3) any SCP- or Wikipedia-derived narrative = mark separately as CC BY-SA (3.0 for SCP; 3.0→4.0 upgrade path then GPLv3 is a plausible chain since BY-SA 3.0 allows "later version" licensing, but confirm), with a `CREDITS.md`/`LICENSES/` folder listing title, author, URL, licence and changes.
- Selling on Steam is compatible with GPL-3.0 and CC BY-SA, but buyers may legally redistribute the GPL/BY-SA components; plan monetisation around convenience/brand rather than exclusivity.
- **Engine risk to verify (outside this note's scope):** if the shipped game must include proprietary ACTION GAME MAKER runtime scripts/assets, a GPL-3.0 licence for the *whole* combined game may conflict with AGM's EULA; keep AGM-proprietary files out of the public repo and clarify "GPL covers my own code only" in README.
- Using PD sources (Q1–Q3) avoids all ShareAlike questions — strongly preferable to SCP for the core plot.

### Gaps
- No official CC or court guidance on whether an entire narrative video game counts as one "adaptation" of BY-SA story text.
- CC BY-SA 3.0 → GPLv3 direct compatibility is not officially declared (only 4.0 → GPLv3).
- AGM EULA terms on redistribution of its runtime/editor components were not reviewed here.

## Q5. Narrative design frameworks (time loops, multi-endings, psychological state, unreliable narrators, mystery, horror pacing, environmental storytelling, meta-fiction ethics)

### Takeaway
A usable toolkit combines: Ashwell's branching patterns (Branch-and-Bottleneck / Gauntlet / Loop-and-Grow) for macro-structure; Emily Short's quality-based/storylet structures for state-driven content (karma, sanity, loop count); Outer Wilds' "knowledge is the only progression" principle for time loops; the Alexandrian Three Clue Rule and node-based design for mystery; tension–release pacing for horror; and explicit, optional content warnings (DDLC precedent) for meta-horror.

### Cited Findings
**Branching structure**
- Ashwell (2015) "Standard Patterns in Choice-Based Games": Time Cave (heavy branching, little re-merging, no state), Gauntlet (one anointed story with optional content/failure branches; creates a hazardous, constrained atmosphere), Branch and Bottleneck (re-merging with heavy state-tracking, often time-governed; e.g., *Long Live the Queen*); patterns are not discrete — [IFWiki copy](https://www.ifwiki.org/Standard_Patterns_in_Choice-Based_Games); [original blog](https://heterogenoustasks.wordpress.com/2015/01/26/standard-patterns-in-choice-based-games/)
- Emily Short, "Beyond Branching" (2016): quality-based narrative (term from Failbetter: storylets unlocked by qualities/state), salience-based narrative (system picks the most applicable content), waypoint narrative — [emshort.blog 2016-04-12](https://emshort.blog/2016/04/12/beyond-branching-quality-based-and-salience-based-narrative-structures/); follow-ups [Storylets: You Want Them (2019)](https://emshort.blog/2019/11/29/storylets-you-want-them/), [Small-Scale Structures in CYOA (2016)](https://emshort.blog/2016/11/05/small-scale-structures-in-cyoa/)

**Time loops / knowledge-gated progression**
- GDC 2020 "Curiosity-Driven Exploration: The Design of 'Outer Wilds'" by Alex Beachum & Loan Verneau (meta design from early 2D prototypes) — [GDC news](https://www.gdconf.com/news/attend-gdc-and-learn-how-outer-wilds-nailed-curiosity-driven-game-design)
- IGS talk "Sparking Curiosity-Driven Exploration Through Narrative in 'Outer Wilds'" — [GDC Vault](https://gdcvault.com/play/1027008/Independent-Games-Summit-Sparking-Curiosity)
- Game Developer on Outer Wilds' loop and curiosity — [Live, die, repeat](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/live-die-repeat-how-i-outer-wilds-i-piques-curiosity-in-an-ambivalent-solar-system); Beachum's IGF interview — [Road to the IGF](https://gamedeveloper.com/design/road-to-the-igf-alex-beachum-s-i-outer-wilds-i-) (search summary attributes to Beachum: the loop fits the idea that "knowledge is the only thing you get" and exists to enable large-scale dynamic systems — quote not verified verbatim)
- GDC 2021 Game Narrative Review on Outer Wilds (student paper PDFs) — [Woodward GNR PDF](https://media.gdcvault.com/GDC+2021/AndrewWoodward_Game+Narrative+Review_OuterWilds+(2).pdf)

**Mystery design**
- Three Clue Rule: "for any conclusion you want the PCs to make, include at least three clues"; similar clues count as one; extra clues cause no problems; only structural revelations must obey the rule; inverse in node design: any three clues should lead to at least one conclusion — [The Alexandrian: Three Clue Rule](https://thealexandrian.net/creations/misc/three-clue-rule.html); [Node-based design](https://thealexandrian.net/creations/misc/node-design/node-design2.html); [Two prongs of mystery design](https://thealexandrian.net/wordpress/8202/roleplaying-games/advanced-node-based-design-part-5-the-two-prongs-of-mystery-design)

**Horror pacing**
- Horror requires a build-up followed by a release; many indie horror games have all build-up and no release — [Game Developer: The Balancing Act of Tension in Horror Game Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/the-balancing-act-of-tension-in-horror-game-design); [GameDev.net blog on pacing](https://members.gamedev.net/blogs/entry/2274395-how-to-use-pacing-and-build-tension-in-a-horror-game)
- GDC session "Defining Horror as Tension" (Silent Hill f team) — [GameDev.net news](https://gamedev.net/news/4735-defining-horror-as-tension-silent-hill-f/)
- Thomas Grip (Frictional) at GDC Europe on Amnesia's focus on evoking emotion — [Game Developer](https://gamedeveloper.com/business/gdc-europe-i-amnesia-i-s-grip-delivers-terrifying-tales-of-immersion); Amnesia: Rebirth's slowly growing dread — [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/amnesia-rebirth-will-have-a-kind-of-horror-that-will-grow-over-time)

**Meta-fiction ethics / content warnings**
- DDLC's opening disclaimer is "a hook … and also a legitimate content warning"; DDLC Plus added optional in-game warnings shown before sensitive scenes — [Team Salvato: Updates to Content Warnings in DDLC Plus](https://teamsalvato.com/blog/updates-to-content-warnings-in-ddlc-plus)
- Warning page lists depression, suicide, self-harm, abuse — [ddlc.moe/warning](https://ddlc.moe/warning)
- Critique: reliance on abuse/mental illness for twists can feel "cheap or crass"; a spoiler-free run is a privilege of players who never needed warnings — [PC Gamer via PressReader](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/pc-gamer-us/20180327/282845076523893)
- Kitfox (Boyfriend Dungeon) on optional spoiler-filled warnings as an effective approach — [Inverse](https://www.inverse.com/gaming/boyfriend-dungeon-villain-controversy-content-warning-kitfox-games-interview)

### Inferences (design kit for a loop-based horror platformer)
- **Macro structure:** Branch-and-Bottleneck per night/loop + persistent "qualities" (loop_count, truth_flags[], sanity, karma) that unlock storylets across playthroughs → multi-ending without exponential branching.
- **Loop rule (Outer Wilds-style):** the *only* thing that persists is player knowledge + a small set of meta flags; gate the true ending behind information (a password, a ritual order, a name) rather than items.
- **Clue graph:** for each structural revelation, place ≥3 clues in different modalities (environmental prop, NPC line, platforming-route secret); track in a spreadsheet/Obsidian graph.
- **Pacing:** alternate chase/hazard rooms (build) with save-shrines/quiet rooms (release); vary loop length to avoid "all build-up".
- **Psychological state:** let sanity alter level geometry/visuals/text (unreliable narration) but never block the critical path; reveal the narrator's unreliability through contradiction between environmental evidence and dialogue (Three Clue Rule helps the player detect it).
- **Meta-fiction:** pre-game warning + optional per-scene warnings toggle; never delete real user files; simulate "file tampering" inside the save system.
- Books commonly cited for structure (general knowledge, not verified this session): Christopher Vogler *The Writer's Journey*; Blake Snyder *Save the Cat!*; Robin D. Laws *Hamlet's Hit Points* (beat analysis — the user's "Hamlet's Hackers" is likely this title).

### Gaps
- Talk contents (slides/video) of the Outer Wilds GDC sessions were not accessed; specific design claims are from promotional blurbs and interviews.
- *Hamlet's Hit Points*, *Save the Cat!*, *The Writer's Journey*, Ron Gilbert's puzzle-dependency charts — not verified in this session.
- Korean-language narrative-design resources (e.g., NDC talks on narrative/horror) — not researched.

## Q7. Concrete "story seeds" (PD motif × trend mechanic × side-scrolling platformer)

### Takeaway
Seven seeds below pair a verifiably PD (or free-motif) source with a 쯔꾸르-style mechanic. Strongest legal positions: Korean anonymous Joseon tales / oral myths (motif-level), Hearn's English *Kwaidan*, M.R. James, Lovecraft pre-1931 works, Kafka's German original, Yanagita's 遠野物語.

### Cited Findings (sources & licence status per seed)
1. **「홍련의 연못 / 사또의 첫날밤」 — 장화홍련전 × time loop + clue graph.** Source plot: magistrates die the day after arriving; a brave one keeps his candle, meets the ghosts, cuts open the "miscarried fetus" to find a skinned rat; twins reborn ending — [Wikipedia summary](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Janghwa_Hongryeon_jeon); 1915 동명서관 edition scan (author unknown) — [ko.wikisource](https://ko.wikisource.org/wiki/%EC%83%89%EC%9D%B8:%EC%9E%A5%ED%99%94%ED%99%8D%EB%A0%A8%EC%A0%84_(%EB%8F%99%EB%AA%85%EC%84%9C%EA%B4%80,_1915).djvu). Licence: anonymous Joseon-era tale → original PD (inference); write own text (don't copy Wikipedia CC BY-SA prose or modern editions).
   - Mechanic: each "night" is a loop; dying resets to the arrival day (previous dead magistrates = your previous runs, shown as ghosts/save slots — meta). Knowledge gates: keep the candle lit (platforming to oil sources), learn to *listen* instead of flee, collect ≥3 clues that the fetus is a rat (Three Clue Rule — [Alexandrian](https://thealexandrian.net/creations/misc/three-clue-rule.html)). 3. **「귀 없는 비파법사」 — Hearn, *Kwaidan* (1904) × body-ink stealth + unreliable perception.** Source: *Kwaidan: Stories and Studies of Strange Things* — [Project Gutenberg #1210](https://www.gutenberg.org/etext/1210) (PD in US; strip PG trademark).
